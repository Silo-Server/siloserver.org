---
title: Profiles, PINs, and shared devices
description: Choose the right household profile and control how a shared device opens Silo.
---

Each profile has its own viewing progress and preferences. The account
password signs the household in; a profile PIN protects entry to one profile.
An administrator's library permissions still apply after you enter a PIN.

## Choose or change a profile

At sign-in, choose your name on **Who's watching?** and enter its PIN if asked.
In the web app, open the profile menu and select **Switch Profile** to return
to the picker. Check the displayed profile before changing preferences or
starting playback on a shared device.

To manage household profiles in the browser, open **Settings > Profiles**
from the primary profile. Open a profile to edit the controls you are allowed
to change. Child profiles and server restrictions can limit these actions.
Ask the account holder for a forgotten PIN; do not delete the profile to
reset it, because deletion also removes its personal data.

## Choose what happens when Silo opens

On Apple devices, open **Settings > General > Profile Selection**.

- **Automatic** reopens the remembered profile. A protected profile can
  reopen without another PIN prompt.
- **Every Time** shows the profile picker whenever you return to Silo.
- **After 1 Hour** and **After 12 Hours** show it after that time away.

On Apple TV, the automatic choice follows the profile selected for the
current Apple TV user. Prefer **Every Time** on a shared TV when you want
people to choose a profile on each return.

These choices apply to that Apple device. They do not remove the profile's
PIN or grant extra access. Android and web launch behavior differ; do not
rely on an Apple launch setting to protect another device.

For a lost device, see [Accounts and signed-in devices](/docs/using-silo/accounts).
