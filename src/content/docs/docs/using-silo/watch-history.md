title: Watched status and resume progress
description: Resume a title, correct watched status, or continue on another device.
---

Use the same profile on each device when you want to continue where you
left off. Another household profile has its own progress, even under the
same account.

## Continue or start over

Open an unfinished item from **Continue Watching**, or open its detail page
and select **Resume**. For a series, check the episode before pressing play.
**Next Up** helps you find the next episode rather than replay a finished one.

To replay from the beginning in the web app, use the detail page's restart
action when offered, or seek to the beginning in the player. A resume point
can move backward when you deliberately replay something.

## Correct watched status

In the web app, open the title's detail page and use its watched/unwatched
action. Check whether you are changing a movie, one episode, a season, or
the whole series before confirming. Marking something watched changes
history; adding it to a watchlist does not.

## Continue on another device

Pause the first player and let it contact the server, then open the title
with the same profile on the second device. If the position looks wrong,
check the profile and edition before seeking manually. Reconnect a device
used offline before expecting its progress elsewhere.

Avoid running the same title in two players while troubleshooting progress:
both can send updates. If the wrong position keeps returning, report the
devices, title, profile, and approximate time to the administrator.

For an existing Plex, Jellyfin, or Emby history, use
[Import your watch history](/docs/import-watch-history).
