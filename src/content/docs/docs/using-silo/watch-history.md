title: Watched status and resume progress
description: Resume a title, correct watched status, or continue on another device.
---

Progress and watched status belong to your profile. Use the same profile on
each device to pick up where you left off. Other profiles on the same account
keep their own progress.

## Continue or start over

Open an unfinished item from **Continue Watching**, or open its page and
select **Play** or **Resume**. **Continue Watching** also shows the next
episode of each series you're watching. To give those episodes their own
**Next Up** row, open **Settings > Home Screen** in the web app and set
**Next up episodes** to **Separate row**. On the same page,
**Show new episodes first** moves a show you've caught up on to the front
when a new episode or season arrives. See
[how Home orders these rows](/docs/home-and-calendar#find-your-next-title).

Until you change them, both settings follow your server's default, which
the person who runs your server sets. Under a setting you haven't changed,
the page shows the value it's using, for example "Using the server default
(off)." Once you've changed one, select **Use server default** beside it to
follow the default again.

In the web app, selecting **Play** on a movie or episode you've started asks
whether to **Resume at** your saved time or **Play from Beginning**.
**Play from Beginning** is also in the title's **More** menu (⋮). Replaying
from the start moves your resume point back.

To clear your resume point on a movie or episode you've started, select
**Reset Progress** in the web app. It's in the menu (⋮) on a
**Continue Watching** card or an episode, or in the long-press menu on a
phone. On a movie or episode page, it's in the **More** menu (⋮), after
**Play from Beginning**. The item leaves
**Continue Watching**, and the next play starts from the beginning. Its watch
history stays, and an item you'd already watched stays watched.

## Remove a title from Continue Watching or Next Up

Open the card's menu and choose **Remove from Continue Watching** or
**Remove from Next Up**:

- In the web app, point at the card and open its menu (⋮). On a
  touchscreen, touch and hold the card instead.
- On iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets, touch and hold the card.
- In the [native Mac app](/docs/native-macos), Control-click or right-click
  the card.
- On Apple TV and Android TV, focus the card, then press and hold Select.

On a title you've started, the menu says **Remove from Continue Watching**.
On the next episode of a show, it says **Remove from Next Up**, in the
**Next Up** row or inside **Continue Watching**.

Removing an episode takes its whole show off **Continue Watching** and
**Next Up**, whichever row you used. The show comes back when you play any of
its episodes again. Removing a movie takes off only that card, and it comes
back the next time you play it. Your resume point and watched status stay as
they are, and the change applies to your profile on every device.

In the web app, a message confirms the removal. Select **Undo** in it to put
the title back. The Apple and Android apps don't have an undo, so play the
title again to bring it back.

## Change watched status

On a phone or tablet, open the title and select
**Watched**.

In the web app, the button's label shows what it will change, for example
**Mark Watched** on a movie or episode, **Mark Season Watched** on a season,
or **Mark Series Watched** on a series. The change applies as soon as you
select it; select the button again to undo it. On a narrow screen, season and
series pages shorten the label to **Mark Watched**.

**Mark Unwatched** clears the item's resume point and removes its watch
history too. To clear only the resume point, use
[**Reset Progress**](#continue-or-start-over) instead.

## Continue on another device

Pause on the first device so it can save your position to the server, then
open the title with the same profile on the second device. If the position
looks wrong, look at the profile and the version you're playing before
seeking. A device used offline sends its progress once it reconnects.

Playing the same title on two devices at once can make the position jump,
because both send updates. If the wrong position keeps coming back,
[report the problem](/docs/report-a-problem) with the devices, title,
profile, and approximate time.

To bring in history from Plex, Jellyfin, or Emby, see
[Import your history from Plex, Jellyfin, or Emby](/docs/import-watch-history).
