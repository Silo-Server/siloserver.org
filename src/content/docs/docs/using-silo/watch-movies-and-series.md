Silo remembers where you stopped for each [profile](/docs/profiles), so pick
your own profile before you watch on a shared device.

## Start or resume

Open a movie or episode. On a series page, the play button starts the next
episode for you.

- **Phone or tablet:** tap **Play**. If you've
  watched part of it, **Continue Watching?** asks whether to **Resume** or
  **Play from Beginning**.
- **Web:** select **Play**. If you've watched part of it,
  **Resume Playback?** offers **Resume at** your saved time or
  **Play from Beginning**. When the title has more than one version, the
  button says **Resume** and **Play from Beginning** is in the **More** menu.
- **Apple TV and Android TV:** choose **Resume**, or **Start Over** to begin
  again.

You can also choose the audio and subtitle tracks on the title's page before
you start.

## Choose a version before playback

Some titles have more than one file, such as a 4K and an HD copy, or a
director's cut. The title's page then shows **Version** to pick the file,
and **Edition** to pick the cut when there's more than one. On Apple TV,
open **Versions**. On iPhone and iPad, **Auto** lets Silo choose.

Audio tracks, subtitles, and running time can differ between versions. After
switching to a different cut, check where playback resumes.

## Controls while you watch

**Web:** move the pointer or tap the picture to show the controls. Besides
pause and seek, you can change the audio track, [subtitles](/docs/subtitles),
**Chapters**, and **Quality**. On a narrow screen, some of these move under
**More player options**. A lower **Quality** can help on a slow connection.

**iPhone and iPad:** tap the picture. **Audio & Subtitles** picks tracks,
**Chapters** appears when the file has chapters, and **Playback Settings**
has **Quality** and more.

**Android phones and tablets:** tap the picture. **Audio and subtitles**
picks tracks, and **Chapters** appears when the file has chapters.
**Quality** switches between versions and only appears when the title has
more than one. On a small screen, these are under **More playback controls**.

**Apple TV and Android TV:** use the remote to show the controls and open
the options panel (**Info and options** on Apple TV). It has tabs for
**Video**, **Audio**, **Subtitles**, and **Chapters** when the file has
them.

## Chapters, intros, and the next episode

Open **Chapters** and choose one to jump to it. **Skip Intro** and similar
buttons appear when Silo knows where that part starts and ends. If one skips
the wrong part, send a [problem report](/docs/report-a-problem) with the
title, episode, and times.

In **Settings > Playback**, choose whether to skip intros, credits, and
recaps automatically and whether to play the next episode.
