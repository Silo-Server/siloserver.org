Silo remembers where you stopped for each [profile](/docs/profiles), so pick
your own profile before you watch on a shared device.

## Start or resume

Open a movie or episode. On a series page, the play button starts the next
episode for you.

- **Phone or tablet:** tap **Play**. If you've
  watched part of it, **Continue Watching?** asks whether to **Resume** or
  **Play from Beginning**.
- **Web:** select **Play**. If you've watched part of it,
  **Resume Playback?** offers **Resume at** your saved time or
  **Play from Beginning**. When the title has more than one version, the
  button says **Resume** and **Play from Beginning** is in the **More** menu.
- **Apple TV and Android TV:** choose **Resume**, or **Start Over** to begin
  again.

You can also choose the audio and subtitle tracks on the title's page before
you start.

## Choose a version before playback

On a title's page, **Edition** picks the cut, such as a director's cut, and
**Version** picks the file, such as a 4K or an HD copy.

**Edition** appears when the title has more than one cut. It opens on the cut
you watched last. If you haven't played the title, it opens on the standard
cut, then the theatrical cut, then the cut with the best file for your
quality settings.

**Version** lists the chosen cut's files when it has more than one. On
**Auto**, the default, Silo picks the best file that fits your
[**Resolution** setting](/docs/preferences#set-playback-quality). With
**1080p**, for example, it picks a 1080p file rather than a 4K one. If every
file's resolution is higher, it picks the lowest. On mobile data, Auto
follows your [mobile data quality](/docs/preferences#quality-on-mobile-data)
instead. Auto never switches to another cut, and choosing an edition sets
**Version** back to **Auto**.

**Play** and **Download** start with the file the page shows. The
**Continue Watching** row on Home resumes the file you last played, even if
the page now shows a different one.

Audio tracks, subtitles, and running time can differ between versions. After
switching to a different cut, check where playback resumes.

## Controls while you watch

**Web:** move the pointer or tap the picture to show the controls. Besides
pause and seek, you can change the audio track, [subtitles](/docs/subtitles),
and **Chapters**. On a narrow screen, some of these move under
**More player options**. The gear button holds **Speed**, from 0.5× to 3×,
and **Quality**. When the speed isn't 1×, the gear button shows it. The
speed lasts until you close the player, and a Watch Party always plays at
normal speed.

**iPhone and iPad:** tap the picture. **Audio & Subtitles** picks tracks,
**Chapters** appears when the file has chapters, and **Playback Settings**
has **Quality** and more.

**Android phones and tablets:** tap the picture. **Audio & Subtitles**
picks tracks, **Chapters** appears when the file has chapters, and
**Quality** changes the quality. The button with three dots opens playback
settings, which also have **Quality**, and **Version** when the title has
more than one.

**Apple TV and Android TV:** use the remote to show the controls and open
the options panel (**Info and options** on Apple TV). It has tabs for
**Video**, **Audio**, **Subtitles**, and **Chapters** when the file has
them.

**Quality** lists **Auto** and lower qualities for the title. Choose **Auto**
to let Silo pick, which plays the original file when your device and
connection can. Your choice applies to what you're watching and doesn't
change your [quality settings](/docs/preferences#set-playback-quality).

## If playback keeps buffering

On **Auto**, Silo lowers the quality when playback keeps stopping to buffer.
**Quality lowered to stop buffering** then appears with **Restore quality**
for 15 seconds. If you picked a quality yourself, or set Silo to ask, it
asks first: **Playback keeps buffering** offers **Lower quality** and **Keep
quality**.
If the title has nothing lower to play, Silo shows **Can't lower quality for
this video**.

After lowering, Silo remembers the slower speed for this device and network
for 24 hours, so the next title starts at the lower quality too. It doesn't
if this connection reached more than 10 times the lowered speed in a speed
test or during playback in the last 7 days. A drop that large more likely
came from the server, such as a disk waking up, so only the title that was
buffering plays lower. While that limit holds a title down, the **Quality**
menu shows **Auto · limited after buffering**.
**Restore quality** goes back to the quality you had and clears the limit.
To clear it later, or to have Silo ask before lowering, see
[Lower quality when buffering](/docs/preferences#lower-quality-when-buffering).

Silo doesn't lower the quality on its own during a Watch Party.

## Chapters, intros, and the next episode

Open **Chapters** and choose one to jump to it. **Skip Intro** and similar
buttons appear when Silo knows where that part starts and ends. If one skips
the wrong part, send a [problem report](/docs/report-a-problem) with the
title, episode, and times.

In **Settings > Playback**, choose whether to skip intros, credits, and
recaps automatically and whether to play the next episode.

Use **Still watching prompt** in the same settings to choose how many
episodes can start on their own in a row, or to turn the limit off. The
default is 3. When the limit is reached, the next-up screen waits for you to
choose **Play Now** instead of starting the next episode, so playback stops
if you've fallen asleep. Pausing, seeking, or starting an episode yourself
starts the count again. On the web, each browser keeps its own setting; in
the apps, each profile on each device does.

## Shuffle a library, series, or collection

Shuffle plays random movies and episodes from a library, a series, a season,
or a collection until you stop. Nothing repeats until everything in it has
played, specials included. Each pick starts from the beginning, not from
where you stopped, and only titles your profile can access are picked.

- In a library of movies or TV shows, select **Shuffle** at the top. In a
  narrow browser window, only its icon shows. On Android phones and tablets,
  it's on the **Library** tab.
- For a series, open its **More** menu. In the web app, choose **Shuffle**;
  do the same on a season's page to shuffle only that season. In the apps,
  choose **Shuffle Series**, or the item for the season you're viewing, such
  as **Shuffle Season 2**. A season needs at least two episodes that can play.
- In a [collection](/docs/collections), select **Shuffle**. On Android phones
  and tablets, it's the shuffle icon at the top of the screen.

Near the end of each title, the next-up screen shows **Up Next at Random**
and what you're shuffling:

- **Play Now** starts the next pick.
- **Pick Another** swaps it for a different random title. The one you skipped
  can still come up later.
- **Stop shuffling** ends the shuffle and returns you to the page where you
  started it.

If auto-play is on, the next pick starts on its own after a countdown, up to
your [still-watching limit](#chapters-intros-and-the-next-episode). To turn
auto-play off, select **Auto-play** on that screen (**Auto-play is On** in
the apps), or change it in **Settings > Playback**. During a shuffle, the
player has no next episode control.
