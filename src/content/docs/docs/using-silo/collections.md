A collection groups titles together. In the web, mobile, or TV app, open
**Collections**, select a collection, then select a title to open it.
Select **Shuffle** on a collection's page to
[play random titles from it](/docs/watch-movies-and-series#shuffle-a-library-series-or-collection)
until you stop.

In the web app, **Collections** has three parts:

- **Your collections**: the ones your profile made. Only you can change them.
- **Shared with me**: collections that other profiles on your account made and
  shared. You can watch them and add them to your Home, but not change them.
- **Server collections**: collections the person who runs your server made for
  each library.

A collection only shows the titles your profile can access. Sharing a
collection with you doesn't give you access to a restricted library.

## Make a collection in the web app

1. Open **Collections** and select **New collection**.
2. Choose what decides what's in it: **Manual** to pick the titles yourself,
   **Smart** to fill it from rules, or **Synced list** to follow a list from
   MDBList or TMDB.
3. Enter a **Name**, then fill it in as described below.
4. Select **Create collection**.

The collection then opens in its editor. Select **Save** after changing it.
Titles you add to, remove from, or drag in a manual collection save right away.

### Pick the titles

For a manual collection, search under **Titles** with **Add a title** and
select **Add**. Drag a title's handle to change the order, or select its remove
control to take it out. The title stays in your library.

### Fill it from rules

For a smart collection, choose under **Rules** which kind of titles it shows
and from which libraries. Select **Add rule**, then pick a field, a condition,
and a value from your libraries. **Live preview** shows the titles that match
as you go. Under **Order**, choose how it's sorted and, if you want a cap,
**Max titles**.

A smart collection keeps itself up to date: titles added to your libraries
later join it when they match. You can create it even if nothing matches yet.

### Follow a list

For a synced list, choose under **The list it follows** where the list comes
from:

- **MDBList**: pick from **Popular picks**, search MDBList, or paste any MDBList
  link. Search only works if the person who runs your server has set it up.
- **TMDB chart**: a chart such as **Trending** or **Top rated**, for movies, TV
  shows, or both.
- **TMDB list**: paste the link to a public TMDB list.

**Sync** sets how often the collection updates. It syncs for the first time
when you create it, and it shows only titles that are already in your
libraries, so a list of 100 trending movies may show far fewer. To update it
before its next sync, open its action menu on **Collections** and select
**Sync now**.

### Hide watched titles

For a manual collection or a synced list, **Show only** hides titles while you
browse, for example the ones you've watched. Nothing is removed from the
collection.

## Share a collection with your other profiles

Under **Where it shows**, turn on **Show to other profiles** and select
**Save**. Every profile on your account then finds the collection under
**Shared with me** and can watch it, but only you can change it. Each profile
sees only the titles that both it and you can access, so a child profile may
see fewer. Nobody else on the server sees it. The switch and **Shared with me**
appear only when your account has more than one profile.

If you turn sharing off, the other profiles lose the collection, including any
Home rows they made from it.

**Show on the Collections tab** also lists the collection on your libraries'
**Collections** tabs, for you and anyone you share it with.

## Add a title to a collection

On a title's page, open **More actions** and select **Add to Collection**.
Tick a collection to add the title; ticking saves right away. Only your own
manual collections are listed. To start a new one, enter a name under **New
manual collection** and select **Create and add**.

## Show a collection on your Home

On **Collections**, open a collection's action menu and select **Add to my
Home…**. In the editor, use **Add as a row** under **Where it shows** to put it
on your Home or a library page. Either way, **Settings > Home Screen** opens
with the row ready to add. For a collection someone shared with you, open it
and select **Add to my Home**.

## Order or delete your collections

Drag a poster under **Your collections** to change the order. To delete a
collection, open its action menu and select **Delete…**. It's removed for you
and every profile you shared it with.

## Collections in the mobile apps

The iPhone, iPad, and Android apps can open collections but can't create
them. Make and fill your collections in the web app.

For a simple reminder, [Watchlist or Favorites](/docs/saved-titles) is
quicker than a collection.
