With **Remote Control**, your phone or tablet picks what plays in the Silo app
on a nearby Apple TV or Android TV. The TV streams from the server, and your
phone or tablet sends the commands. Both devices need to be on the same home
network, with Silo open on the TV. If the TV isn't signed in yet, [set it
up](/docs/tv-sign-in) first.

## Connect and play

1. Tap **Remote Control** (the remote icon) at the top of the Home screen or a
   title's page.
2. Choose the TV from the list.
3. Open a movie or episode and tap **Play**. It plays on the TV instead of
   your phone or tablet.

On iPhone and iPad, tapping **Remote Control** on a movie or episode page
starts that title on the TV you choose.

While you're connected, tapping the remote icon opens a menu:
**Remote Control** shows the remote, **Choose TV** switches to another TV,
and **Turn Off Control Mode** goes back to playing on your phone or tablet.

## Use the remote

The remote has play and pause, skip back and forward, and volume, plus
**Audio**, **Subtitles**, **Quality**, and **Speed** when the TV's player
offers them.

Tap **More options** (**...**) on the remote to **Choose a Different TV**.
**Stop Playback** stops the title on the TV, and **Disconnect** ends the
connection.

## If your TV isn't in the list

Open Silo on the TV so it's on screen, and make sure your phone or tablet and
the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. A guest network can keep them from
finding each other. If the connection drops, tap **Remote Control** again once
the network is back.
