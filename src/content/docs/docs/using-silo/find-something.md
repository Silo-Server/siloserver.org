---
title: Find a movie or series
description: Browse libraries, narrow results, and explore cast and crew.
---

Start in a library when you know what kind of media you want. Use search
when you know a title or person. The steps here use the web app.

## Browse a library

1. Open the main menu and choose a library.
2. Open a title to read its details. For a series, choose a season and episode.
3. Use the library's filter and sort controls to narrow a long list. Clear
   the filters if an expected title disappears.
4. Select **Play** or **Resume** on a playable title.

In **Settings > Libraries**, **Remember library pages** keeps your library
view choices for later visits. A saved filter can explain why two profiles
appear to show different results.

## Search and explore people

Open search from the main navigation and enter the title or name. Check the
year when several results share a title. On a movie or series detail page,
select a cast or crew member to open their page and filmography. Use its
movie/series filter to narrow the list.

Discovery results can describe titles that are not playable on your server.
A release date, poster, or cast credit does not mean the media file is present.
Use [Requests](/docs/using-silo/requests) if your server allows them.

## When something is missing

Check the selected server, profile, library, and filters first. If a title is
still absent or has the wrong details, send the administrator its name,
year, and library. They can check file scanning, identification, and access.
Changing your profile's language does not correct a wrongly matched title.

For less specific browsing, try [Home, For You, and Calendar](/docs/using-silo/home-and-calendar).
