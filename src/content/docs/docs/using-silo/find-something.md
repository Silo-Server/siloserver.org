Browse a library when you know what kind of media you want, or search when
you know a title. These steps work the same on the web and on Apple and
Android phones and tablets.

## Browse a library

1. Choose a library from the app's navigation.
2. Open a title to read its details. For a series, choose a season and episode.
3. Use the library's filter and sort controls to narrow a long list. If a
   title you expect disappears, clear the filters.
4. Select **Play** or **Resume**.

In the web app, **Remember library pages** under **Settings > Libraries**
returns each library to the last tab, sort, and filters you used on that
profile and device. A saved filter is often why two profiles show different
results.

## Search and explore people

Open **Search** and enter a title. In the web app you can also search for
people by name. When several results share a title, pick by year.

On a movie or series page, select a cast or crew member to open their
**Filmography**. Choose **Movies** or **Series** to narrow it.

Some results, such as upcoming releases or a person's other work, may not be
on your server. If your server allows it, you can [request](/docs/requests)
them.

## When something is missing

Look at the selected server, profile, library, and filters first. If the
title still isn't there, or its details are wrong, tell the person who runs
your server. Include the title, year, and library. They can check
[library scanning and access](/docs/manage-libraries) or
[metadata matching](/docs/metadata).

For less specific browsing, try [Home, For You, and Calendar](/docs/home-and-calendar).
