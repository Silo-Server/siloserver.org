Add titles you want to watch to your **Watchlist**, keep the ones you love in
**Favorites**, and rate titles to record your opinion. All three belong to
your active profile.

## Save a title

On a phone or tablet:

1. Open the movie or series.
2. Select **Favorite** (the heart) or **Watchlist** (the bookmark).
3. Select it again to remove the title.

In the web app:

1. Open the movie or series.
2. Select the heart to add it to your favorites. Select it again to remove it.
3. To use the watchlist, open the **More** menu (⋮) and choose
   **Add to Watchlist** or **Remove from Watchlist**.
4. Select a star rating.

On a narrow screen, series, season, and episode pages move the heart and
stars into the **More** menu as **Add to favorites** and **Your rating**.

Open **Favorites** or **Watchlist** from the main menu to see what you've
saved. If a change fails to save, check your connection and try again.

Series in your favorites or watchlist can send you
[new-episode notifications](/docs/notification-inbox), depending on your
settings. Ratings also shape your [For You](/docs/home-and-calendar)
suggestions.

For your own named groups of titles, use [Collections](/docs/collections).
