Use **Watchlist** for something you want to watch, **Favorites** for something
you want to keep close, and a rating for your opinion. These belong to your
active profile.

## Save a title

On Apple and Android phones or tablets:

1. Open the movie or series detail page.
2. Select **Favorite** (the heart) or **Watchlist** (the bookmark).
3. Select the same control again to remove the title.

In the web app:

1. Open the movie or series detail page.
2. Select the heart to add it to **Favorites**. Select it again to remove it.
3. For a watchlist entry, open the detail page's more-options menu and choose
   **Add to Watchlist**. The same menu offers **Remove from Watchlist** later.
4. On a movie or series detail page, select a star rating. On a narrow
   screen, open the more-options menu and use the stars under **Your rating**.

Open **Favorites** or **Watchlist** from the main menu to find saved titles.
Wait for a change to finish before leaving the page. If it fails, check the
connection and retry; a failed save will not reliably appear on another device.

Adding a series to one of these lists can also make it eligible for new-episode
[notifications](/docs/notification-inbox), depending on your preferences.
Saving a title does not request it, download it, or mark it watched.

For your own named groups of titles, use [Collections](/docs/collections).
