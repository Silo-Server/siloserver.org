Start with your profile's preferences for choices you want to reuse. Use a
device override when one screen or sound system needs something different.
For example, a bedroom TV may need an audio delay that your phone does not.

On Apple and Android phones or tablets, **Settings** includes **Playback**
and **Subtitles** controls. Use the web app for the pages described below;
device-specific controls and their placement differ between apps.

## Change a profile preference in the web app

Open **Settings** from your profile menu, then choose the relevant page:

- **Playback:** quality defaults, spoken and metadata languages, skipping,
  and the next episode.
- **Subtitles:** language, behavior, and appearance.
- **Home Screen:** your row order and visibility.
- **Appearance**, **Navigation & Cards**, or **Accessibility:** the web
  interface controls shown on those pages.

Changing metadata language asks Silo to display available metadata in that
language. It does not change the movie's spoken audio. Web interface choices
also do not guarantee the same appearance in native apps.

## Check a device override

1. Open **Settings > Your Devices** and choose the affected device.
2. Check its quality, HDR, audio sync, or subtitle sync value.
3. Change only the setting relevant to the problem, or use its reset control
   to return to the inherited value.
4. Start playback on that device and check the result.

An explicit device value can take precedence over a profile default. A
specific title's saved track choice can also explain why it plays differently
from another title. Check the chosen audio/subtitle track during playback
before changing the whole profile.

**Forget** removes the device's saved settings and hides it until it is used
again. It does not sign that device out. For a lost device, follow
[Accounts and signed-in devices](/docs/accounts).
