You need the server address and an account on that server. Use the account
you created during setup, or the invitation or credentials supplied by the
person running it. Silo accounts belong to that server; an account on one
server does not sign you in to another.

## Get an app or open your browser

The quickest way to try Silo is to open the server address in a browser.
You do not need to install anything to watch there.

For a phone or tablet, follow the Apple or Android installation steps in
[Choose an app](/docs/choose-an-app), then return here to sign in.
For a television, install the TV app and follow
[Sign in on your TV](/docs/tv-sign-in).

## Accept your invitation

1. Open the invitation link your administrator sent you.
2. Check the server and account details. Choose a password, confirm it, and
   select **Create account**.
3. Follow the sign-in prompt. If the link has expired or was already used,
   sign in with your existing account or ask for a new invitation.

If your administrator already created your account, skip the invitation and
use the credentials they gave you. Do not assume the server allows public
registration.

## Sign in

### In a browser

1. Open the server address, not siloserver.org.
2. Enter your username and password and select **Sign in**. If your server
   offers another sign-in provider, use the method your administrator assigned.
3. Choose your profile on **Who's watching?** Enter its PIN if asked.

### On an iPhone, iPad, or Android phone or tablet

1. Open Silo and enter the full server address, including `https://` and any supplied port.
   Connect to the server.
2. Enter your Silo username and password, then select **Sign in**.
3. Choose your profile and enter its PIN if asked.

Your **account** holds the sign-in. A **profile** holds one person's progress,
preferences, and restrictions. Use your own profile to keep your viewing
separate from other people in the household.

## Play something

Open a library, choose a movie or episode, and select **Play**. For an item
you have already started, choose **Resume**. You should see playback begin
with sound. [Playback controls](/docs/watch-movies-and-series)
cover audio, subtitles, and quality.

An empty library is not a reason to reinstall the app. Check your selected
server and profile, then check your account's
[library access](/docs/manage-accounts) in the server settings if you
manage the server, or contact the person who does.
If the server address will not open in a browser on the same device, check
the address and network before retrying your password.

Setting up a television? Follow [Sign in on your TV](/docs/tv-sign-in).
