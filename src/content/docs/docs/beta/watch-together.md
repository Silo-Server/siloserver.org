:::caution[Beta]

:::

Watch Party lets people on the same Silo server pick a movie or episode and
watch it together. Everyone's playback stays in step, and the host controls
play, pause, and seeking.

| App | Watch Party |
| --- | --- |
| Web | In the sidebar |
| iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV | Turn it on in Settings first |
| Android phone, tablet, and TV | Turn it on in Settings first |

People can join from any of these apps, so one party can include web, Apple,
and Android viewers.

## Turn it on in the apps

Watch Party is off in the apps until you turn it on. The switch is for this
device only.

- On iPhone or iPad, open **Settings** and turn on **Watch Party** under
  **Extra Features**.
- On Apple TV, open **Settings > Server** and turn on **Watch Party** under
  **Extra Features**.
- On an Android phone, tablet, or TV, open **Settings** and turn on **Watch
  Party** under **Experimental**.

**Watch Party** then appears in your profile menu, and a title's **More**
menu gets a **Watch Party** option. The apps show it only when your server
supports Watch Party.

## Start a party

In the web app:

1. Open **Watch Party** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Host picks** to choose the title yourself, or **Everyone votes**
   to let everyone suggest titles and vote.
3. Select **Create Watch Party**.

In the apps, open **Watch Party** from your profile menu, then choose **Start
a party** to pick the title yourself or **Start a party and let everyone
vote**.

To start with a title already picked, open its page first. In the web app,
open the **More actions** menu, select **Start a Watch Party**, then select
**Create party & copy invite**. For a series, you can change the episode
before you create it. In the apps, choose **Watch Party** from the title's
**More** menu.

## Invite people

Share the room code, or the invite link if you're the host. In the web app,
select **Invite link** at the top of the room. In the apps, select the code
at the top of the party to see the code and a QR code, which opens the invite
on a phone. On iPhone, iPad, and Android phones, **Share invitation** sends
the invite.

People you invite need a profile on the same server. Each person watches with
their own profile, so profile restrictions still apply: an invite doesn't let
someone watch a title their profile can't open.

## Join a party

- In the web app, open **Watch Party**, enter the code, and select **Join
  Watch Party**. An invite link opens the party after you sign in and choose
  a profile.
- On iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, or an Android phone or tablet, open **Watch
  Party**, enter the code or paste the invite link, and select **Join
  party**.
- On Android TV, open **Watch Party**, select **Join with a code**, and enter
  the code.

On iPhone or iPad, an invite link opens in the browser first. Select **Open in
the Silo app** to join from the app.

To get back into a party you left, open **Watch Party** again and select
**Rejoin**. Each device remembers your recent party for a day.

## Choose what to watch

In a party where the host picks, the host searches for a movie or series and
chooses what to play. For a series, pick the season and episode. Guests can
suggest titles, and the host can queue one of them.

In a party where everyone votes, anyone can suggest titles and vote for them.
The host starts the one with the most votes, or any other suggestion.

Guests can mark themselves **I'm ready**. The host selects **Start for
everyone** when people are ready, or starts anyway. Watch Party plays movies
and episodes only.

## During the party

Only the host can seek. To let guests play and pause too, the host turns on
**Guests can pause** in the web app, or sets **Play & pause** to **Host and
guests** in the party options in the apps. On Apple TV and Android TV, the
setting is **Playback controls**, with **Guests can play and pause**. Silo
remembers your choice for the next party you host.

While the party plays:

- Everyone plays at normal speed, and the next episode doesn't start on its
  own. When a title ends, everyone returns to the party to choose the next
  one.
- If someone's video stops to buffer, the party waits for them briefly. If
  they're still not ready, it keeps playing and they catch up.
- Silo doesn't lower your quality on its own. If your connection can't keep
  up, Silo offers to lower it for you. You can still change the quality
  yourself.

The host can send everyone back to the party to choose something else, with
**Stop for everyone** in the web app or **Return everyone to lobby** in the
apps.

## Leave or end a party

Guests can leave at any time with **Leave** in the web app or **Leave party**
in the apps. When the host leaves, the party waits two minutes for them to
come back, then ends.

The host can end the party for everyone with **End room** in the web app or
**End party** in the apps. An ended party can't be rejoined, so start a new
one and send a new invite.

If you join from a second device with the same profile, the party moves to
that device and the first one leaves.

If a party won't open, make sure you're signed in to the right server and
profile.
